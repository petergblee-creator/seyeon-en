    FAQ

    Quick answers

    We already have a local accountant. Can you review their work?

    Yes. A review of your current books and filings is a common starting point, and the first consultation is free.

    Do you work with our HQ's tax advisors?

    Yes. We coordinate directly with your HQ controller and tax advisors on intercompany, transfer pricing and reporting items.

    How is the fee structured?

    A fixed monthly fee based on agreed scope, confirmed in writing before we start. No surprise billing.

    How fast do you respond?

    Within one business day (KST). Urgent filing matters are handled the same day.

    Can you handle labor or legal issues?

    Yes, through partner law firms, coordinated through us so you keep a single point of contact.
